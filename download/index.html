Install QSkip App

Add QSkip directly to your home screen without needing an app store

iOS (iPhone / iPad)

Safari Browser

  1. 1 Open qskip.ge in the Safari browser.
  2. 2 Tap the Share button .
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  4. 4 Tap Add in the top-right corner.
Open in Safari

Android

Chrome Browser

  1. 1 Open qskip.ge in Google Chrome.
  2. 2 Tap the three dots (⋮) menu.
  3. 3 Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. 4 Confirm by tapping Install.
Open App in Chrome
